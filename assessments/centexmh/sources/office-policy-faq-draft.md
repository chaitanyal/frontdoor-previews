## 1. Who do you see, and what kind of care do you provide?

We provide psychiatric care for adults 18 and older, with a focus on medication management. Supportive discussion may be part of your visit. For ongoing psychotherapy or counseling, we recommend working with a separate therapist.

Source/status: S2/S4; safe paraphrase. Combines age eligibility and treatment scope.

## 2. How do I schedule my first appointment?

Complete the new-patient request, then call (512) 964-6992 and select option 1 to discuss scheduling. Once your appointment is scheduled, the office will provide intake paperwork to complete before your visit.

Source/status: S4; safe paraphrase. Existing scheduling workflow retained.

## 3. Do you accept insurance, and when is payment due?

We accept insurance, including traditional Medicare, and offer self-pay options. Medicare Advantage plans are not accepted. Copays, coinsurance and outstanding balances are due at your visit. Let us know if your coverage changes, and check your benefits with your insurer. Call (512) 964-6992 for plan-specific coverage and self-pay rates.

Source/status: S5 and P1 p. 1, Payment 1–4; safe paraphrase. No dated dollar amounts or comprehensive network claim.

## 4. How do I request a medication refill?

Ask your pharmacy to send the refill request electronically or by fax at least five business days before your last dose. If you have run out or have two or fewer doses left, call the office for assistance. Some refills, including stimulant prescriptions, may require an appointment.

Source/status: P1 p. 2, Refill Requests 1–5 and Controlled Medications 1; safe paraphrase. No refill guarantee, legal assertions or prescription-expiry claims.

## 5. What if I need to cancel, reschedule or arrive late?

Please give at least 48 hours’ notice when canceling or rescheduling. Late cancellations and missed appointments carry fees. If you are running late, call as soon as possible; arriving more than five minutes late may mean rescheduling your visit.

Source/status: P1 p. 1, Appointment Policies 1–3; safe paraphrase. Uses explicit requested notice; does not invent treatment of the ambiguous 24–48-hour window. Fees omitted pending currency confirmation.

## 6. Can I have a telemedicine appointment?

We offer telemedicine appointments. Contact the office to discuss which appointment format is available for your visit.

Source/status: S2/S3; safe paraphrase. Existing format availability retained, no eligibility guarantee.

## 7. What should I do if I need help after hours?

Established patients can call (800) 544-6444 for after-hours assistance from the on-call nurse. For a medical or psychiatric emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Source/status: P1 p. 2, After-Hours Resources 1–3; S6 live resources page confirms number and established-patient restriction. Safe paraphrase. No claim of routine weekend office hours.

## 8. Can you help with FMLA or disability paperwork?

FMLA and disability forms may require an additional appointment so your clinician can gather the information needed. There is a separate fee for each form. Contact the office to discuss the paperwork and fees.

Source/status: P1 p. 2, Forms for Disability/FMLA 1–3; safe paraphrase. No approval or turnaround guarantee; dated dollar amounts omitted.
