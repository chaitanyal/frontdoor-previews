Physician-Led Care

Collaborative Expertise Across Specialties

CARMAhealth brings together a team of more than two dozen practitioners spanning general psychiatry, child and adolescent psychiatry, addiction medicine, neurology, and family medicine. Our physician-led model means your care is guided by board-certified psychiatrists and supported by experienced psychiatric nurse practitioners.

Whether you need a psychiatric evaluation, ongoing medication management, or advanced treatments like TMS or ketamine therapy, our practitioners work as a team to find the right path for you. With offices in Austin, Dripping Springs, and Coral Springs, plus telehealth across three states, quality psychiatric care is always within reach.
