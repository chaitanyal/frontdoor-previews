Why CARMAhealth

What Sets Us Apart

We offer a unique practice that combines board-certified practitioners, next generation treatments for hard-to-treat conditions, integrated primary care, and broad insurance acceptance.

01

Board-Certified Practitioners

State leaders in psychiatry, addiction medicine, and behavioral health

Your treatment is directed by board-certified practitioners, including psychiatrists (adult, child, and adolescent) and addiction medicine practitioners. Our founder is Co-Chair of the Texas Medical Association Committee on Behavioral Health and a Clinical Associate Professor at UT Dell Medical School.

02

Next Generation Treatments

FDA-cleared treatments for hard-to-treat conditions

We offer next generation treatments such as TMS, Spravato, and supervised ketamine for hard-to-treat conditions like depression. These treatments offer meaningful improvement in as few as 4 to 6 weeks.

03

Integrated Primary Care

Primary care that understands behavioral health and recovery

Our primary care team is trained in behavioral health and substance use, providing routine medical care that fully accounts for your mental health and recovery journey.

04

Insurance Plans

Most advanced treatment clinics are cash-only

We accept Aetna, Ambetter, BCBS, Cigna, Humana, Sendero, United Healthcare, and more. Advanced psychiatric care shouldn't require paying out of pocket.
