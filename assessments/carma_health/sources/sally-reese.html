Meet Your Practitioner

Whole-Person Care

Sally Reese is a dual-certified Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP-C) and Psychiatric-Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP-BC) at CARMAhealth. Her unique combination of primary care and psychiatric expertise allows her to take a whole-person approach to patient care, addressing both physical and behavioral health needs in a single practice.

Sally earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing from the University of Oklahoma before completing her Master of Science in Nursing at The University of Texas at Austin in 2010. She returned to UT Austin for a Post-Master's Certificate in 2015, earning her psychiatric nursing specialty. She holds board certification from the American Nurses Credentialing Center.

A published author in the Journal of Nurse Practitioners, Sally is deeply committed to serving those in need. She has provided care through medical missions in Costa Rica and Juarez, Mexico, reflecting her passion for preventive health and service to underserved communities. At CARMAhealth, she focuses on psychiatric evaluation, medication management, and integrated primary care for patients navigating mental health and addiction-related conditions.
