Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

FrontDoor Health respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or request a website preview.

Information We Collect

When you contact us or request a personalized website preview, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Practice name
  • Website URL
  • Information you voluntarily provide

We also collect limited website usage information to understand how visitors interact with our website and improve our services.

Analytics and Cookies

FrontDoor Health uses cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand visitor behavior, and measure the effectiveness of our marketing.

We use Google Ads Conversion Tracking to understand whether visitors who click our advertisements later submit a website preview request or contact us.

Google may place cookies on your device to measure advertising performance. These cookies do not provide us with access to your personal accounts or passwords.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain website functionality and measurement.

How We Use Your Information

We use information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Prepare personalized website previews
  • Improve our website
  • Measure marketing effectiveness
  • Communicate with prospective clients

We do not sell your personal information.

Healthcare Disclaimer

FrontDoor Health provides website design and marketing services for independent medical practices.

FrontDoor Health does not provide medical care, diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice.
